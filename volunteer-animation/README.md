# إيثار وعطاء — Volunteer Awareness Animation

Awareness animation for the National Agency for Youth Voluntary Efforts, built from the agency's own character designs and logo.

- `volunteer-awareness.mp4`: final video, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 34 s, with a synthesized background music track.
- `index.html`: the animation source. Open it in a browser and press **Play**. Each scene is driven by one `render(t)` timeline function, so timings, captions and colors are easy to change.
- `assets/`: the 20 character poses cut from the sprite sheet, the logo badge, and the Cairo font.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | Sunrise over the city: the sky warms up, windows switch off, birds fly across | مع إشراقة كل صباح… تولد فرصة جديدة للعطاء |
| 6–13 s | Planting trees: volunteers arrive, saplings grow, the watering can pours | نزرع اليوم… ليستظل الغد |
| 13–20 s | The light turns red, the car stops, and a volunteer helps an elderly man across | نمدّ أيدينا لكبارنا |
| 20–26 s | Relief boxes are filled, closed and stacked | معًا نصنع الفرق |
| 26–34 s | All four volunteers gather, and the logo spins in with a glow, rays and sparkles | الوكالة الوطنية للجهود التطوعية للشباب |
